Tank & Trench
Tank & Trench is AI Search for Septic & Excavation: the agency that makes a contractor the answer when a homeowner searches Google or asks an AI assistant who to call.
Category we claim: AI Search for Septic & Excavation. Never "an SEO agency."
One-line pitch: "When someone in your county searches or asks ChatGPT who pumps septic tanks, the answer should be you. We build that."
The promise: more phone calls, more form fills/contact form reach outs. Not rankings, not impressions.
Who we serve: owner-operated septic pumping, installation, inspection and excavation companies. Often run by the owner and an office manager, with no marketing staff.
Our method: the Answer Map
Every client gets an Answer Map: their services, towns, equipment and common homeowner questions, mapped and connected so Google and AI assistants know exactly what they do and where. It is built once, owned by the client, and grows every month. —-> this is our way of appearing Scientific - look at Wordlift —> David Riccitelli → We take his approach, but dig in on Septic/Excavation → they will have never seen something so specialized just for them, which is where we likely convert 10+ (hopefully more) clients. I assume this can be done with AI, but in a systemized way.
