Septic Tank Baffles

What are the inlet and outlet baffles?

The inlet baffle directs incoming wastewater into the tank. The outlet baffle lets liquid flow onward while helping keep floating material in the tank.

Source EPA: How Septic Systems Work, in Financing Septic Systems
