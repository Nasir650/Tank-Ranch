Legal Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Shellback Labs LLC, doing business as Tank & Trench (“we,” “us”), runs tankandtrench.com. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you fill out a form on our site, we collect what you enter: your name, company, phone number, email, town, county, state, and any message. When you call us, we receive your phone number. When you text us, we receive your phone number and the content of your message.

Business information from public sources

We build our mailing lists from publicly available business information, including Google Maps listings, company websites and state contractor license lists. This can include a business’s name, address, phone number, website, reviews, ratings, and the first name of an owner or contact where it is publicly listed.

We use this information to research how a business appears in search results and AI answers, to create a personal check page for that business, and to mail it a postcard. Personal check pages are hidden from search engines and are reached only through the link or QR code on the postcard.

AI checks

To see which companies AI assistants recommend, we send questions such as “Who’s the best septic company in [town]?” to AI providers like OpenAI (ChatGPT) and Anthropic (Claude). These questions contain only a town and public business details. We save each answer with the date we received it. We do not send information you submit through our forms to AI providers for these checks.

Calls and texts

We use call tracking (CallRail) to count calls from our website and mailings. For each call we record the date, time, length, caller’s number, and the page or mailing that led to it.

If you give us your phone number, you agree that we may call or text you about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to stop texts at any time, or HELP for help. Mobile numbers and text opt-in consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use our site, including pages viewed, clicks, and session recordings. These tools use cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and Google offers an opt-out browser add-on for Google Analytics.

How we use information

  • To respond to your request and send your free AI check or video audit
  • To provide services to our clients
  • To measure which mailings and pages bring in calls and form submissions
  • To improve our site and services
  • To comply with the law

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with:

  • Service providers who run parts of our business for us: website hosting (Cloudflare), form processing, call tracking (CallRail), postcard printing and mailing, analytics (Google, Microsoft), and AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic)
  • Our fulfillment team, who work on client accounts under confidentiality obligations
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect our rights
  • A buyer or successor, if our business is sold or merged

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and lead information for up to 24 months after our last contact, and client information for as long as we work together plus any period the law requires. We delete information sooner if you ask.

Your choices

  • Stop postcards: Email us your business name and address and we will remove you from future mailings within 30 days. A mailing already printed may still arrive.
  • Remove your check page: Ask and we will take it down.
  • See or delete your information: Email us and we will respond within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under state law. Contact us to use them.

Children

Our site is for businesses and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect their information.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information. No method of storing or sending data online is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the effective date.
